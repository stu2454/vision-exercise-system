Vision Exercise System · early prototype

Sit to Stand

This uses your webcam to watch you stand up from a chair and sit back down, and counts the repetitions. It takes about five minutes.

Nothing you record is sent anywhere

The camera picture stays in this browser tab. It is never uploaded, never recorded to a file, and never seen by anyone else. Everything — including the counting — happens on your own computer.

Close the tab at any time and the camera switches off immediately.

What you will need

  1. A sturdy chair without wheels Ideally with a firm seat. Please do not use a stool or anything that might slide.
  2. Room to stand up safely Something stable within reach if you would like it. Stop if you feel unsteady.
  3. Space to put the computer about 2–3 metres away Far enough that your whole body, head to feet, fits on the screen.

Step by step

How to use it

  1. Allow the camera Your browser will ask. Nothing works without it, and nothing leaves your computer.
  2. Position the chair and the computer Sit down facing the screen. The screen will tell you if you need to move back or closer, in large letters you can read from the chair.
  3. Raise one arm to begin Bend your elbow, hand up beside your head, and hold it there for about a second. A bar fills to show it has seen you. Then put your arm down — you have three seconds before it starts counting.
  4. Stand up and sit down, at your own pace Stand up fully, pause a moment, then sit back down with control. The number on screen counts each one. There is no rush and no target you have to reach.
  5. Raise both arms to finish While still sitting. Hold for about a second and it will stop and show your total.

If something looks wrong

It may miss a repetition, or count one you did not mean. That is what we are trying to find out, so please tell Stuart what happened rather than assuming you did it wrong.
